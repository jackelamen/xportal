Getting Started

Welcome to Your Portal

xPortal gives you full visibility into your projects: review deliverables, follow progress, and stay in sync with your team - all in one place, in real time.

Signing In

xPortal uses passwordless sign-in. There's nothing to remember - just your email address.

1

Enter Your Work Email

On the sign-in page, type the email address your project team has on file, then click "Email me a sign-in link."

2

Check Your Inbox

A sign-in link arrives within moments. It's valid for 15 minutes.

3

Click the Link

Click the link in the email. You're taken straight into your workspace - no password to set or remember.

4

Sign In Again Later

Every sign-in works the same way: enter your email, get a new link. Links expire after 15 minutes, so if yours doesn't work, just request a new one.

Tip: Didn't get the email? Check your spam folder, and confirm you used the exact email address your project team invited.

Home

Your Dashboard at a Glance

Home is the first thing you see after signing in - it greets you by name and surfaces what needs you right now.

Needs Your Attention

Files your team is waiting on, deliverables awaiting your review, invoices coming due, unread messages, and meeting times that need your response. When nothing's pending, you'll see "You're all caught up."

Your Projects

Opening a Project

Click any project on Home, or a project card, to open its detail page. Each project has five tabs.

Tab What It Shows
Overview The project hub: key links, your team and ours, what's actively being worked on, and the decision log.
Deliverables Work submitted for your review - approve it or request changes.
Documents Contracts, agreements, brand assets, reports, and any files your team has requested from you.
Progress The project journey: which phases are done, active, blocked, or upcoming, plus what's happened and what's coming up.
Messages A single conversation thread with your project team.

The Phase Timeline

Near the top of Overview and Progress, a segmented bar shows every phase at a glance.

  • Green - finished
  • Indigo - in progress now
  • Red - blocked, needs attention
  • Grey - hasn't started
Tip: A blocked phase means your team has flagged something that needs your input - check the Overview tab's "We're working on" section, or send a message to ask what's needed.

Key Results

Some projects track numeric goals - the metrics this project is judged by. Green means the goal is being met or beaten, amber means it's close, red means it's off target.

The Decision Log

A running record of key decisions made on the project, including every deliverable approval - so there's never a question about what was agreed and when.

Reviewing & Approving Deliverables

Deliverable Status

Status Meaning Your Action
Pending Review Submitted by your team, waiting on your decision Review it, then approve or request changes
Approved You've approved this version - it's final No action needed
Changes Requested You've asked for revisions; the team is working on an update Wait for the revised version

Approving a Deliverable

1

Open the Deliverable

From the Deliverables tab, click "View the work" to review the submitted file or link.

2

Make Your Decision

Choose Approve or Request changes.

3

Confirm Approval

Approving is final - it locks the deliverable and notifies your team immediately. There's a confirmation step before it's locked in.

Requesting Changes

If something isn't right, choose Request changes and describe exactly what should be different - the note is required, and your team sees it verbatim, so be specific.

Tip: Reference specific pages, sections, or timestamps. A precise note gets you a faster, more accurate revision.

Previous Versions

Every deliverable keeps its history. Use "Show previous versions" to compare what changed between rounds.

Documents & Files

Document Categories

Documents in the Documents tab are grouped into five categories:

  • Contracts
  • Agreements & NDAs
  • Reference
  • Brand assets
  • Reports

Files We Need From You

Sometimes your team needs a file only you can provide. These show up as file requests at the top of the Documents tab - upload directly there to fulfill the request.

Uploading a Document

1

Open Documents

Select the Documents tab from within a project.

2

Choose a File

Click "Choose file…" and select the file from your device.

3

Add a Title (Optional)

If left blank, the filename is used as the title.

4

Upload

Click Upload. Each document shows who uploaded it and when.

Calendar & Scheduling Meetings

Calendar

The Calendar tab shows everything on a timeline: meetings, milestones, phase end dates, and invoices due.

  • Meetings
  • Milestones
  • Phase ends
  • Invoices due

Booking a Meeting

1

Open Schedule

Click Schedule in the sidebar.

2

Enter a Topic

Describe what the meeting is about.

3

Pick a Length

Choose how long the meeting should run.

4

Choose a Time

Pick from the available slots shown.

5

Submit

Your request shows as "Awaiting operator response" until your team confirms.

If a New Time Is Proposed

Your team may propose a different time. You can Accept it, or suggest a different time yourself.

Canceling a Meeting

Open the meeting from your Schedule and choose Cancel. You'll be asked for a reason before it's confirmed.

Billing & Invoices

Your Billing Overview

The Billing tab totals what's outstanding, what's been paid this year, and what's due next.

Invoice Status

Status Meaning Your Action
Paid Payment received Keep for your records
Unpaid Issued, payment not yet received Pay according to your contract terms
Overdue Past its due date Contact your team to arrange payment
Disputed Under review after a dispute - escalation is paused Wait for your team's follow-up in the dispute thread

Disputing an Invoice

1

Open the Invoice

Click the invoice you have a question about.

2

Click Dispute

Click the Dispute button on the invoice detail screen.

3

Explain What's Wrong

Be specific - e.g. "We were quoted 12 hours for this phase, but the invoice shows 20." This is required.

4

Submit

The invoice pauses and a thread opens with your team. They'll follow up with you directly.

Downloading a Copy

Every invoice has a PDF link for your records.

Account & Preferences

The client portal keeps account controls minimal - everything lives at the bottom of the sidebar.

Switch Language

Click the EN / 한국어 toggle to switch the entire portal between English and Korean. Your choice is saved to your account.

Switch Theme

Toggle between light and dark mode. The sidebar itself always stays dark, regardless of this setting.

Sign Out

Click your name, then Sign out, to end your session. You'll need a new sign-in link next time.

Note: There's no password to manage and no separate profile page - sign-in is always via an emailed link.

FAQ & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create a password?

No. xPortal uses passwordless sign-in - you'll always get a fresh link by email.

My sign-in link expired - now what?

Go back to the sign-in page and request a new one. Links are valid for 15 minutes.

Can I edit project details myself?

No - project details are managed by your team. Use the Messages tab to request a change.

How current is the information I see?

Everything updates in real time as your team makes changes - no need to refresh or wait.

Once I approve a deliverable, can I undo it?

No - approving is final and notifies your team immediately. If something's wrong after approving, send a message to your team.

Can other people at my company get access?

Yes - ask your project lead to send them an invitation. Each person signs in with their own email.

What browsers work with the portal?

Any current version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.

Troubleshooting

I'm not receiving the sign-in email

  • Check your spam folder
  • Confirm you're using the exact email your team invited
  • Wait a minute, then request another link
  • Ask your team to confirm you're set up as a portal user

A document won't download

  • Check your internet connection
  • Make sure your browser allows downloads
  • Try a different browser
  • Check your downloads folder - it may have saved without a visible notification

The portal feels slow

  • Check your connection speed
  • Close unused browser tabs
  • Clear your browser cache
  • Try a different device
Need more help? For anything not covered here, send a message to your project team from the Messages tab, or contact your account manager directly for urgent matters.
