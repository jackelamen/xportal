Getting Started

Login & Access

To access the Admin Portal, navigate to your organization's operator sign-in page and log in with your email and password.

Important: The Admin Portal does not use a separate password. Sign in with the same email and password as your xPM account. There's nothing new to set up.
1

Enter Your Work Email

Use the same email address you use for your xPM account.

2

Enter Your xPM Password

Use the same password as your xPM account. It is not a separate credential.

3

Click Sign In

You'll be redirected to the Admin Portal dashboard.

Tip: Forgot your password? Click "Forgot Password" on the login page to reset it via email.

Interface Layout

The admin portal features a clean, organized interface designed for efficiency:

  • Sidebar Navigation: Access main sections on the left
  • Top Bar: Shows current date, status indicators, and user menu
  • Main Content Area: Displays active section and data
  • Breadcrumbs: Navigate back through your path

Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard provides a comprehensive snapshot of your organization's status and recent activity.

Key Dashboard Sections

Key Performance Indicators

View real-time KPIs including project count, team size, and completion rates at a glance.

Recent Projects

Quick access to your most recently active projects with status indicators.

Team Status

See team member availability and their current task assignments.

Reading Your Dashboard

  • Color-coded status indicators show project health (green = on track, amber = needs attention, red = blocked)
  • Click any project card to view detailed information
  • Use the filter options to customize your view
  • Data refreshes automatically every few minutes
Tip: Customize your dashboard by pinning frequently accessed projects to the top.

Managing Projects

Creating a New Project

1

Navigate to Projects

Click "Projects" in the left sidebar.

2

Click "New Project"

Use the primary button at the top of the projects list.

3

Fill Project Details

Enter project name, description, client, start/end dates, and assign a lead.

4

Define Phases

Break your project into phases with milestone dates and deliverables.

5

Save Project

Click the "Create Project" button to complete setup.

Project Status Types

Status Description Action Required
On Track (Green) Project progressing as planned Continue monitoring
Needs Attention (Amber) Minor issues or timeline concerns Review and address issues
Blocked (Red) Significant blocker preventing progress Immediate intervention needed

Editing Project Details

  1. Click on a project from the list
  2. Click the pencil icon or "Edit" button at the top
  3. Modify project information as needed
  4. Update timeline or phases if needed
  5. Click "Save Changes" when complete
Warning: Archiving a project cannot be undone. Archived projects can be restored from the Archive section, but use caution.

Project Timeline & Phases

Each project is divided into phases with specific deliverables and milestones:

  • View the phase timeline on the project details page
  • See upcoming milestones and their due dates
  • Track progress against scheduled dates
  • Add or modify milestones as needed

Task Management

Creating Tasks

Tasks are the smallest unit of work in your projects. Create tasks to assign specific work to team members.

1

From a Project

Click a project, then "Add Task" in the tasks section.

2

Enter Task Details

Title, description, priority, and due date are required fields.

3

Assign Team Member

Select a team member from the dropdown to assign the task.

4

Set Dependencies (Optional)

Mark other tasks that must be completed before this one starts.

5

Save Task

Click "Create Task" to complete.

Task Status & Updates

  • To Do: Task not started
  • In Progress: Task being worked on
  • In Review: Task completed, awaiting review
  • Done: Task completed and approved
  • Blocked: Task cannot proceed due to dependencies

Viewing All Tasks

Access the "All Tasks" view from the sidebar to see every task across all projects:

  • Filter by project, assignee, status, or due date
  • Sort by any column
  • Bulk update task status
  • Export task list as CSV
Tip: Use the workload view to see task distribution across your team and identify overallocated members.

Team Management

Adding Team Members

1

Open Team Section

Click "Team" in the left sidebar.

2

Click "Add Member"

Use the primary button to start adding a team member.

3

Enter Email Address

Provide the team member's email address.

4

Assign Role

Choose their role: Admin, Lead, or Member.

5

Send Invite

Click "Send Invite" to notify them of their account.

Team Member Roles

Role Permissions
Admin Full access to all features, can manage team and settings
Lead Can manage projects and team members within their projects
Member Can view and work on assigned tasks and projects

Team Spaces

Organize team members into spaces for better collaboration:

  • Create spaces by team, department, or project type
  • Assign team members to multiple spaces
  • Set space-specific permissions and defaults
  • Use spaces to organize dashboards and reports

Managing Team Workload

The Workload view helps you understand team capacity:

  • See total tasks per team member
  • Identify members who are over or under-allocated
  • Rebalance workload by reassigning tasks
  • View team velocity and capacity trends

Glossary

Terms used throughout the admin portal, in the order you'll typically encounter them.

Term Meaning
Workspace The top-level container for your organization's projects and team. Created once, on first login.
Space A sub-grouping of projects inside a workspace - by team, department, or client - used to organize dashboards and access.
Project A body of work with its own timeline, phases, tasks, and team assignments.
Phase A stage within a project's timeline (visible on the project Gantt view). Phases can contain milestones.
Milestone A specific checkpoint or target date inside a phase.
Task The smallest unit of work - has a title, assignee, priority, due date, and status.
My Tasks The view showing only tasks assigned to you, across all your projects.
Workload The view showing task distribution across team members, used to spot over- or under-allocation.
CRM The contact and client relationship tracking section of the portal.
Archive Storage for completed or removed projects and tasks. Archived items can be restored.
Task status To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done, or Blocked - where a task stands right now.
Role Admin, Lead, or Member - determines what a team member can see and change.

Troubleshooting & Support

Common Issues

Can't Log In

  • Check that you're using the correct email address
  • Verify your password is correct (case-sensitive)
  • Use "Forgot Password" to reset your password
  • Ensure your account has admin privileges

Tasks Not Appearing

  • Check applied filters-you may have filtered out the tasks
  • Verify the task's project is visible to your space
  • Ensure you have permission to view the project

Team Member Can't See Their Tasks

  • Confirm the task is assigned to their account
  • Verify they have access to the project
  • Check that their team member status is active, not archived

Slow Performance

  • Clear your browser cache
  • Disable browser extensions that may interfere
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  • Try accessing from a different browser
